# Nissan X-Trail 2026 × Fakto Auto — brand

## Voice
- Three adjectives: confident, calm, outdoorsy
- We say: short Lithuanian lines, verbs first ("Pažiūrėkite iš naujo.")
- We never say: prices or offers (not in this test), superlatives we can't prove ("geriausias", "pigiausias")

## Look
- Colours (hex): black #0a0a0b, white #f4f4f2, Nissan red #c3002f (thin lines and accents only)
- Fonts: Barlow Condensed (headlines, uppercase), Inter (body), both Google Fonts / OFL
- Logo files: `assets/fakto-logo.svg`, `assets/nissan-logo.svg` (from faktoauto.lt)
- Visual style: dark and cinematic, real car photography, thin technical callout lines, lots of negative space
- Colours and fonts also go in `brand.css`, which every design uses.

## Proof
- Fakto Auto is an authorised Nissan dealer (Vilnius). Its awards (e.g. best Nissan dealer in the Baltics) are on faktoauto.lt. Re-check the year and wording before using them.
- Car facts: only those in `brief.md` → Facts we may state.
