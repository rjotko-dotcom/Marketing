# X-Trail 2026 launch ad: script + storyboard v1 (draft)

30 s master in 16:9 (YouTube, web, showroom screens), plus a 9:16 recut for Reels/TikTok, and a 15 s cutdown that keeps shots 1, 3, 5 and 6.
Voiceover in Lithuanian. Music: one cinematic pulse track, free-licensed. Put the credit here once it's picked.

## Hook options (first 2 s)

1. **Doors open (picked):** dawn, Fakto showroom. The glass doors slide open and headlights switch on inside. No words, just sound.
2. **Grille macro:** extreme close-up of the new mesh grille as light sweeps across it. Text: "Naujas veidas."
3. **Silhouette:** car fully dark in the showroom, then the lights come on one by one in time with the beat.
