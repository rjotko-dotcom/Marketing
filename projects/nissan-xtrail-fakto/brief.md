## Legal / platform rules for car ads

- **EU/LT car advertising:** promotional material for a new car must show the official WLTP fuel consumption and CO₂ figures for the model. Put them on the end card, legible.
- No invented prices, offers, financing rates, awards or customer quotes. Financing ads need the full legally required info (APR etc.) — only if Fakto gives us an actual offer.
- **Nissan brand use:** logo, imagery and the X-Trail name follow Nissan's brand guidelines. The correct source is Nissan's dealer asset portal, which goes through Fakto. Nissan press-site photos are licensed for editorial use, not for advertising.
- **AI footage:** the car in every shot must match the real 2026 car. Use image-to-video from real photos, never text-to-video ("a Nissan SUV…"), which invents grilles, badges and wheels. Meta and TikTok require you to label realistic AI-generated content in some cases; check on upload.
- The dealership shown must be the real Fakto showroom (photo or video of it), not an AI-invented building with their logo on it.
