# Weekly review

1. Read `brief.md` (goal number), `calendar.md` (what was planned) and `log.md` (what ran).
2. Take numbers only from what the user pasted or a connected analytics source. If a number is missing, list it as missing — do not estimate it.
3. Write a short review at the top of `log.md`:
   - Goal: current vs target, on/off pace.
   - Table: what ran · spend · result · cost per result.
   - **Stop** (no signal after a fair test), **Double down** (best cost per result), **Try** (one new experiment, with its stop rule).
4. Update `calendar.md` for next week.
5. Tell the user in 5 lines or fewer: pace, best thing, worst thing, next week's one change.

Small numbers lie: under ~30 conversions, call results "early signal", not winners.
